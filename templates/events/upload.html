    What happens to your recording

    1. Google Teachable Machine listens to it in your browser and produces its own scores.
    2. The file and those scores are sent to the server, which checks the format, size, integrity, duration, sampling rate, channels and that it contains sound.
    3. The Python model analyses every one-second window on the server.
    4. The two results are compared and the alert rules decide severity, alerts and whether a person should review it.

    The two models never see each other's answer: GTM finishes before the upload starts.
